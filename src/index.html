What this model captures

Dropout-limited output target, a reference-derived LM1117 current clamp and rated-current approximation for the other parts, a nominal 40 µs control response and 0.08 Ω output resistance (20–80 µs and 0.04–0.12 Ω in the assumption sweep), capacitor ESR, and a stepped current load. ngspice solves the circuit afresh for every proposal.

What these results do not establish

These are lab-authored behavioral models, not manufacturer transistor-level models. LM1117 dropout follows a nominal TI reference curve; the other parts use linearly scaled datasheet endpoints; control dynamics and current clamp are assumptions. ESR stability, startup, noise, production variation, PCB layout, pinout and thermal protection require further analysis and hardware tests.

Junction temperature = ambient + estimated dissipation × user-selected board θJA. It is a steady-state estimate, not electrothermal simulation. Accuracy is checked as a separate specification budget; its full corner variation is not simulated.

Offline versus live AI

Offline mode uses a visible deterministic candidate ranking. Live mode calls NVIDIA Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory to propose the next experiment after receiving previous failures. Neither mode can assign the evaluator’s verdict.
